Methodology

Polywatch surfaces statistical patterns in public trading data. A flagged wallet hasn't been shown to have traded on non-public information; many flags will be lucky guesses, hedges, or new users. Wallets are pseudonymous Polymarket accounts.

Which markets

Four times a day the scanner checks open Polymarket markets with at least $10k of volume where a small group could know the outcome before the public: executive and government appointments, corporate actions, rulings, central-bank decisions, military action, award results, and what public figures will say or post. Crypto price, weather and sports markets are excluded.

What gets scored

Every buy at a price of 0.25 or less since the previous scan, including resting limit orders. Buys are summed per wallet, market and outcome. Positions under $1,000 are ignored. Wallet age and diversification are measured as of the first flagged trade.

Data

Polymarket Gamma API (markets) and Data API v2 (trades and wallet activity). All public and read-only. The first scoring rules were tuned on the November 2026 "Jensen Huang rejoins NVIDIA" market.
